# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily the owner: a developer working on AI agents, RAG and backend engineering who reads engineering blogs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cloudflare, GitHub) and keeps personal study notes. The site is their own reference shelf first, published openly second. Typical use: looking something up again, re-reading a note mid-task, or adding a new reading note after finishing an article. Occasional visitors are other developers arriving from search or a shared link.

## Product Purpose

A personal knowledge site with five shelves: 文章 (reading notes on engineering blogs, each linking to the original), 知识库 (own notes by topic), 仓库 (GitHub repositories worth following), Skills (own AI Skills) and 工具箱 (bookmarked tools). Success means finding a past note in seconds, reading long technical text comfortably, and the site still feeling right after years of additions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, unhurried. The voice of an engineer's notebook: plain statements, no hype. Visual reference is the Anthropic website and its Engineering blog, specifically its typographic system (bold sans headlines over serif reading text), near-monochrome warm-white palette, two-column layout with an illustration rail on the left, hairline-separated title lists with right-aligned dates, and restrained black geometric illustrations. Borrow the system, never the identity: no Anthropic name, logo, proprietary fonts or illustrations.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing kits: grids of identical rounded cards, gradient washes, icon-heading-text tiles.
- AI-template chrome: tracked uppercase eyebrows over every section, numbered section markers, decorative side-stripe borders.
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins on every block and parallax effects.
- Dark-by-default "tech" themes with neon accents.

## Design Principles

1. Reading first: long Chinese technical prose and code must be comfortable before anything is decorative.
2. Structure over ornament: hierarchy comes from type size, weight and rules, not boxes and color.
3. One quiet signature: the geometric illustrations are the only expressive element; everything around them stays disciplined.
4. Findable in seconds: search, tags and the archive matter more than the homepage hero.
5. Honest attribution: reading notes always point to the original article and separate the source's claims from the owner's view.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard operation of search and dialogs. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Light and dark themes both meet contrast. Chinese text set with adequate size (16px+ body) and line height.
